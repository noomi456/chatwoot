# Public Brand Assets

## Purpose

Own browser-, device-, PWA-, and customer-visible static identity for ChatRing Conversation Core.

## Ownership

- Logos and wordmarks.
- Favicons and device icons.
- PWA manifest identity.

## Local Contracts

- Customer-visible assets must use ChatRing identity and ChatRing-owned artwork.
- Preserve filenames used by upstream templates where doing so avoids compatibility churn.
- Do not remove required open-source license or third-party notice files.

## Work Guidance

- Keep SVGs valid and raster icons at their expected dimensions.
- Avoid renaming public asset paths unless templates and compatibility behavior are migrated together.

## Verification

- Parse `manifest.json` successfully.
- Render the wordmark and representative favicon/icon sizes.
- Verify browser, PWA, login, dashboard, widget, and survey surfaces after deployment.

## Child DOX Index

- No child DOX files currently required.
